Want to jump right in? Join our Discord server and start with the !guide command! Join Discord

Getting Started

The Trial Junkie Discord bot is your gateway to generating trial accounts for various streaming and subscription services. Here's how to get started:

  1. Join our Discord server
  2. Authenticate with the bot using !register or link your web account
  3. Check your subscription tier with !tier
  4. Start generating trials!

Trial Generation Process

Generating a trial with our bot is simple and straightforward. Here's a step-by-step breakdown:

Step 1: Select a Service

Use the !hit [service] command to generate a complete trial for a specific service.

Example: !hit netflix or !hit hulu

Step 2: Wait for Account Generation

The bot will generate all necessary credentials:

  • Identity: Name, address, and location information
  • Email: A disposable email for verification
  • Phone Number: A virtual phone number for verification (if needed)
  • Payment Method: A virtual card for the trial subscription

This process typically takes 10-30 seconds depending on service complexity.

Step 3: Receive Your Account Details

Once complete, the bot will provide:

  • Login information (username/email and password)
  • Subscription details (plan type, trial period)
  • Expiration date (when the trial will end)
  • Cancellation instructions (to avoid charges)

Step 4: Access Your New Account

Simply log in to the service using the provided credentials and enjoy your trial!

All credentials are saved in your !stash for later reference.

Automation Features

For supported services, you can use the !trip [service] command to have the bot automatically create the account for you.

The bot will use browser automation to:

  • Navigate to the service website
  • Fill out registration forms
  • Verify email and phone (if required)
  • Enter payment information
  • Complete the signup process

When complete, you'll receive the login details just like with the !hit command.

Individual Agent Commands

Need just a specific piece of information? Use the !dose command to generate individual components:

  • !dose identity - Generate just a person's identity
  • !dose email - Create a disposable email
  • !dose card - Generate a virtual card
  • !dose phone - Get a virtual phone number

Managing Your Trials

Use these commands to manage your trials:

  • !stash - View all your saved credentials
  • !rehab - Clear your saved data (requires confirmation)
  • !tier - Check your subscription status and limits

Command Reference

  • !guide - Display help information
  • !hit [service] - Full trial setup
  • !dose [agent] [platform] - Single agent generation
  • !trip [service] - Automated trial creation
  • !stash - View saved credentials
  • !rehab - Clear user data
  • !agents - View available agents
  • !plans - View subscription plans
  • !pay [service] - Make a payment
  • !tier - Check subscription tier
  • !referral - Get/manage referral code
