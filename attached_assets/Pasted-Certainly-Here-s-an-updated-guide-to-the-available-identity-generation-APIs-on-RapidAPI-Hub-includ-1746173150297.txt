✅ Integration Tips

Authentication:
For all the above APIs, include the following headers in your requests:

x-rapidapi-host: The host provided in the API documentation.

x-rapidapi-key: Your RapidAPI key. 


Selecting an API:
Choose the API that best fits your requirements:

For comprehensive identity generation, consider the Dummy Identity Generator API or Fake Identity Generation.

For simpler identity data, the Random Identity Generator or Identity Generator may suffice. 


Note:
Always ensure that the generated identities are used in compliance with legal and ethical guidelines, and only for testing purposes.
